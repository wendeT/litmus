Verified: genuine performance gap, not a scoring artifact. mistral-small-3.2 obtains substantially lower accuracy than gpt-oss-120b on word_swapping (2.4%), character_insertion (24.9%), and character_swapping (29.7%). Manual inspection of raw model outputs confirms this is not an evaluation error: quote-wrapped responses are normalized correctly prior to scoring, and expected outputs were independently verified against the source word and character positions. Most incorrect responses are near-correct rather than unrelated to the target (edit distance at most 2 for the character-level tasks, at most 4 word-level edits for word_swapping), indicating mistral-small-3.2 consistently identifies an incorrect position when performing the manipulation rather than failing to attempt the task. The higher accuracy of gpt-oss-120b (above 75%) on the same tasks is consistent with precise positional manipulation being a scale-sensitive capability in language models.
